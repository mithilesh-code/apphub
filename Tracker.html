Your 90-day journey

Date is used for phase guidance. Changing it never deletes your logs.

PHASE 01

Build the foundation

Days 1–30

Keep the starting barrier low.

  • Leadership: 5–10 min/day
  • English: 5–15 min/day
  • Night reflection: 2–5 min/day
  • Observe actual screen time, then reduce slowly
  • Aim to practice on ~20 days, without perfection pressure
PHASE 02

Strengthen consistency

Days 31–60

Practice skills in real situations.

  • One 3-minute recorded leadership speech each week
  • English speaking towards 15 min/day
  • Gentle movement ~3 days/week
  • Keep a defined social-media window
  • Reflect weekly and adjust one habit
PHASE 03

Lead by example

Days 61–90

Build identity through repeatable action.

  • Prepare a 5-minute executive-style update
  • Study a cybersecurity leadership scenario each week
  • Continue weekly and monthly reviews
  • Protect family time, health and sleep
  • Notice what is sustainable long-term

Your practical rhythm

Adapt freely to your real schedule.

Morning: Leadership practice; choose one priority.
During work: One optional 25-minute focus block.
Evening: Phone-free family time and English speaking.
Night: Ask what went well, what remained unfinished, and the next small step.

When motivation is low: open a 5-minute timer and begin. The objective is to start, not to feel inspired first.
